# Asheville, NC Airbnb Listings – BUS 446 Final Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Airbnb listings in Asheville, North Carolina, to identify the key factors associated with higher estimated annual rental revenue. Our goal is to provide data-driven recommendations for property owners and potential investors.

## Business Problem
Our goal is to identify which property characteristics, pricing strategies, geographic locations, and host-related factors are associated with higher Airbnb revenue. This information can help property owners and potential investors make more informed investment decisions.

## Dataset
Our dataset contains 2,865 Airbnb listings in Asheville, North Carolina, collected from Inside Airbnb.

The dataset includes 90 variables covering:
- Property characteristics
- Nightly pricing
- Geographic location
- Host information
- Customer reviews
- Estimated annual revenue
- Occupancy

## Methods and Tools
We used the following tools and methods:
- **Tableau:** Created visualizations to explore property types, revenue by ZIP code, geographic distribution, nightly pricing, and customer satisfaction.
- **Excel:** Conducted regression analysis to examine relationships between property characteristics and estimated annual revenue.
- **GitHub:** Organized and shared project materials.

## Key Findings

### 1. Property Types and Revenue
Larger properties generally demonstrate higher revenue potential. Seven-bedroom properties had the highest median estimated annual revenue at approximately $99,387.

### 2. Location and Revenue
ZIP code 28801 had the highest median estimated annual revenue at approximately $20,544, followed by ZIP code 28806 at $19,780.

### 3. Geographic Distribution
Airbnb listings are concentrated in several geographic clusters throughout Asheville. Higher-revenue properties appear across multiple areas.

### 4. Nightly Price and Annual Revenue
The analysis shows a generally positive relationship between nightly rental prices and estimated annual revenue. However, higher nightly prices do not guarantee higher earnings.

### 5. Customer Satisfaction
Average guest ratings remained close to 4.9 out of 5 across different levels of host experience.

### 6. Regression Analysis
Our regression analysis identified several factors associated with estimated annual revenue:
- Nightly price: +$48.53 per $1 increase.
- Guest capacity: +$3,796 per additional guest accommodated.
- Customer ratings: +$17,016 per one-point increase.
- Superhost status: +$16,372.
- Bedrooms: -$4,436 per additional bedroom, holding other variables constant.

The regression model explained approximately 33.2% of the variation in estimated annual revenue.

## Business Recommendations
Based on our findings, we recommend that property owners and investors:
- Prioritize property size and guest capacity.
- Evaluate geographic revenue patterns and local competition.
- Establish competitive nightly pricing.
- Focus on maintaining high customer satisfaction and strong host reputations.
- Consider occupancy, property characteristics, and operating costs when evaluating investment opportunities.

## Conclusion
Successful Airbnb investment decisions require balancing property characteristics, geographic location, competitive pricing, and customer satisfaction rather than relying on a single factor.
